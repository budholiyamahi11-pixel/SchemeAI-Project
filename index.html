About SchemeAI Initiative

SchemeAI is an independent citizen-centric project built to bridge the awareness gap between eligible Indian beneficiaries and public welfare schemes.

Every year, thousands of crores in public welfare funds remain underutilized because deserving citizens are unaware of schemes or find eligibility rules convoluted. SchemeAI translates complex ministerial criteria into a simple, actionable digital checker.

Statutory Disclaimer

SchemeAI is a public informational tool. It is NOT affiliated with, authorized by, or an official service of the Government of India or any State Government. Final eligibility, entitlement, and sanction of any scheme are decided exclusively by the competent designated statutory authorities upon formal submission of required applications and authentic documentation.
